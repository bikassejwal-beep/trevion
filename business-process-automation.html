Business process automation FAQs

What is business process automation?

It is the use of software, scripts, or connected tools to carry out defined, repeatable steps in a business workflow with less manual effort.

Which tasks are suitable for automation?

Tasks with repeated steps, consistent inputs, and clear expected outputs are usually good candidates. Processes involving frequent judgement or exceptions may need human review.

Does automation remove the need for human review?

Not always. Important decisions, unusual cases, and sensitive data should have appropriate checks. The level of review depends on the workflow and its risks.
