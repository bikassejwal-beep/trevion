Design basis

Equal friction method: the duct is sized so friction stays at or below the friction rate, and velocity is only checked. Velocity method: the duct is sized so velocity stays at or below the max velocity, and friction is only reported. Round sizes go up to the next standard size, rectangular sizes to the next 50 mm.

Size and check Main

Type the side your space allows. The other side is sized for you with the method chosen above. Type in the other side yourself to test a size you want, and the status tells you if it passes and why. Flow and length come from the highlighted row of the table. Height 300 mm is an example.

Damper by loss coefficient

ΔP = K × ½ρv², with v taken on the damper face area (πD²/4 for round, width × height for rectangular) and ρ = 1.204 kg/m³. Rectangular size 300 × 200 mm is an example.

Filter size

Velocity = airflow ÷ ((W1 + W2 + W3) × height). Enter 0 for a width you do not use.

Plenum box size

Width = larger of the equipment opening and the filter bank, plus insulation on both sides. Height = larger of the filter height and the equipment opening height, plus insulation on both sides. Depth = equipment opening height + access panel width + insulation on both sides. Connections must fit inside the inner width and height.
