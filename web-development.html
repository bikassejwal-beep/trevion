Website development FAQs

Can you build a website for a small business?

Yes. A project can be scoped around the business's goals and needs, such as service information, contact details, and useful calls to action.

Will the website work on mobile phones?

Responsive layout is part of the design approach, with content and navigation arranged to suit different screen sizes. The finished site should be reviewed on the devices and browsers relevant to its audience.

Does SEO-friendly design guarantee a top Google position?

No. Clear page structure and descriptive content help search engines understand a site, but rankings depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.
